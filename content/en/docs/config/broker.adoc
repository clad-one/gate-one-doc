The optional `cladone-auth` broker configuration. Secrets are read from the environment; a `*Env` field names the variable.

| no

| [[field-tenants-rules]] `rules`
| array of <<config-tenants-rules,object>>
| no
| when/then policy rules: test the incoming token's claims (when) and grant rights (then), including derived roles (rolesFrom) and copied claims (copyClaims). Evaluated on every login; results are not persisted on the user.

[[config-tenants-rules]]
==== tenants.rules

A when/then policy rule: test the incoming token's claims and grant rights. Evaluated on every login; results are not persisted on the user.

[cols="2,2,1,5",options="header"]
|===
| Field | Type | Required | Description

| [[field-tenants-rules-when]] `when`
| array of <<config-tenants-rules-when,object>>
| yes
| Conditions, combined with logical AND. A single condition object is also accepted as shorthand.

| [[field-tenants-rules-then]] `then`
| <<config-tenants-rules-then,object>>
| yes
| Rights granted when every condition matches.

|===
